1. Overview

NeoGen is a fully automated, AI-powered double-entry bookkeeping SaaS platform. All features are delivered through software and AI (Claude API). No manual work, consulting, or human intervention is provided.

Enterprise plans are designed for organizations requiring multi-user access, advanced compliance features, ESG reporting, and dedicated support.
